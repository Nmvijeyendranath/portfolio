STAGE 01 / SOURCE SESSION

A user authenticates with a real web source — Reclaim-backed session proofs let the system reference what the source actually showed, without handing over the whole document.

INTERACTIVE ARCHITECTURE WALKTHROUGH — NOT A LIVE VERIFICATION. AI EXTRACTION ALONE IS NOT PROOF THAT A CLAIM IS TRUE.

THE CHALLENGE

Prove a specific credential without handing over an entire document or adding a custom API for every data source.

THE APPROACH

Explore Reclaim-backed web-session proofs, structured claim extraction, keccak256 claim hashes in the MST registry, and a recruiter/auditor portal that queries verifySkill() for supported claims.

MY TAKEAWAY

Trust depends on the whole chain: the source proof, claim validation, contract logic, and what a verifier actually checks.
